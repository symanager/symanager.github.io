Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Short version: SyManager doesn't collect, store or share any of your data. The app talks directly to your own NAS, and everything it saves stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. SyManager has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services of its own. The developer never receives your data.

Your NAS

SyManager connects directly from your device to the Synology NAS you sign in to, using the same interface as DSM's web pages. What you see and change in the app (files, settings, users, logs and so on) goes only between your device and your NAS.

What's stored on your device

  • NAS addresses and account names you add, so you can switch between them.
  • Passwords, only if you choose to save them. They're kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only (protected by Face ID when it's set up), aren't included in backups or iCloud, and are only ever sent to your own NAS to sign in.
  • App settings, such as layout choices.

Removing a NAS in the app deletes its saved password. Deleting the app removes everything else.

QuickConnect

If you sign in with a QuickConnect ID, the app asks Synology's QuickConnect servers where to find your NAS, and the connection may be relayed through Synology's servers, just as in Synology's own apps. That's covered by Synology's privacy policy. Signing in with a local address or your own domain doesn't use QuickConnect.

Permissions

  • Local network: to reach your NAS on your Wi-Fi.
  • Face ID: to unlock saved sign-ins. Face data is handled by iOS and is never available to the app.

Links

Some screens link to Synology's websites or open your NAS's web pages in Safari. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Any in-app purchase is handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Demo mode

Demo mode uses sample data built into the app and doesn't connect to any NAS.

Children

SyManager isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
