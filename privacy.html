Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the SyManager mobile application (the "App", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the App on iPhone or iPad. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information. The App does not require an account, and it does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party crash reporting tools. We do not operate any servers that receive data from the App.

2. How the App Works

The App connects directly from your device to the Synology NAS that you choose to sign in to. Information displayed or changed in the App, including files, settings, user accounts and system logs, is transmitted only between your device and your NAS. This information is not sent to us.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • NAS addresses and account names that you add to the App;
  • Passwords, only if you choose to save them. Saved passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device, are protected by Face ID where available, and are not included in device backups or synced through iCloud;
  • App preferences, such as display settings.

This information remains on your device. Removing a NAS from the App deletes its saved password, and deleting the App removes all information stored by the App.

4. Third-Party Services

Synology QuickConnect. If you sign in using a QuickConnect ID, the App contacts Synology's QuickConnect service to locate your NAS, and your connection may be relayed through Synology's servers. This service is provided by Synology Inc. and is subject to the Synology Privacy Statement. QuickConnect is not used when you sign in with a local network address or your own domain name.

Apple. Any purchases made in the App are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

External links. The App may contain links to third-party websites, such as Synology's websites or your NAS's web interface. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these websites.

5. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions. You can change them at any time in your device's Settings.

  • Local Network: used to connect to your NAS on your local network.
  • Face ID: used to unlock saved sign-in information. Biometric data is processed by iOS and is never accessible to the App.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

7. Data Security

Connections to your NAS use the security settings you configure, including HTTPS where enabled. Saved passwords are protected by the iOS Keychain. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Support page.
