Support

SyManager's community lives on GitHub. Anyone can read it; you need a free GitHub account to post.

Report a bug

Open a bug report. Please include your SyManager version, device, NAS model and DSM version, and the steps that cause the problem.

Suggest a feature

Suggest a feature, or add a 👍 to an existing one.

Questions

Ask in Discussions.

Security problems

Please report them privately rather than in a public issue.

Keep your NAS private

Issues and discussions are public. Never post passwords, 2FA codes, your QuickConnect ID, public IP addresses or domain names, and hide serial numbers and user names in screenshots.

Testing with TestFlight?

You can also send feedback right from TestFlight: take a screenshot in SyManager and tap Share Beta Feedback.
